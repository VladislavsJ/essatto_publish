# Building regulations by room type: Finland and USA

Collected on 15 November 2026 for the plan-check concept. Every entry names the document, the clause and a link, and says how it was checked.
Quotes are copied from the texts saved in `sources/`. English renderings of Finnish clauses are ours, not official translations.

**This is not legal advice and not a complete list.** It covers what could be read from official texts in the time available. Three limits matter most:

- Finland: no national rule was found for classroom floor area per pupil, and no clause that names kitchens for fire detection.
- USA: building rules are state law. The federal accessibility standard (ADA) applies everywhere; fire and egress rules are shown from Washington State's published code, and two entries from the model code are marked NOT checked.
- Guidance is not law. The English school area guideline (BB103) used elsewhere in this project is guidance and is not repeated here.

14 Finnish entries, 11 US entries; 2 not checked against a primary text.

## Finland: the clauses

### FI-848-2017-38: fire warning system

- Document: Fire safety of buildings, decree 848/2017
- Clause: 38 § and Table 12
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: any room in a school, classroom, kitchen, corridor
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/848 (copy: `sources/fi_848_2017.xml`)

> Asuntojen, majoitustilojen, hoitolaitosten, päivähoitolaitosten, päiväkotien ja muiden varhaiskasvatuksen tilojen sekä koulujen on oltava varustettu tarkoituksenmukaisella laitteistolla, joka varhaisessa vaiheessa ilmoittaa alkavasta palosta.

In English (our rendering): Schools, among others, must be equipped with an appropriate device that gives early warning of an incipient fire. Table 12: up to 250 pupils a mains-connected smoke alarm; 251 to 500 a fire alarm system; over 500 a fire alarm system connected to the emergency response centre.

Limit: Requires the system for the building. It does not name rooms.

### FI-848-2017-34: exit width

- Document: Fire safety of buildings, decree 848/2017
- Clause: 34 § Uloskäytävän mitat
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: corridor, exit, stair
- Requirement: 1,200 mm wide, 2,100 mm high
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/848 (copy: `sources/fi_848_2017.xml`)

> Uloskäytävän leveyden on oltava vähintään 1 200 millimetriä ja uloskäytävän korkeuden on oltava vähintään 2 100 millimetriä.

In English (our rendering): An exit must be at least 1,200 mm wide and 2,100 mm high. Exceptions follow in the same section (for example a second exit of 900 mm where the escape area holds at most 60 people).

### FI-848-2017-32: travel distance to the nearest exit

- Document: Fire safety of buildings, decree 848/2017
- Clause: 32 § and Table 10
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: any room, classroom, hall
- Requirement: 45 m in general for 'other spaces'; 30 m where there is only one exit
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/848 (copy: `sources/fi_848_2017.xml`)

> Kulkureitin pituus lähimpään uloskäytävään ei saa olla vaaraa aiheuttavan pitkä.

In English (our rendering): The travel distance to the nearest exit must not be dangerously long. Table 10 gives the maximum by use of the escape area.

Limit: Table 10 has footnotes and higher values for areas with a smoke-detection fire alarm or sprinklers; read the table before using a single figure.

### FI-848-2017-35: door swing

- Document: Fire safety of buildings, decree 848/2017
- Clause: 35 § Poistumiseen käytettävät ovet
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: hall, classroom, exit
- Requirement: opens in the direction of escape when more than 60 people use the door
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/848 (copy: `sources/fi_848_2017.xml`)

> Ovien on avauduttava poistumissuuntaan jos kyseessä on asunnon kerrostaso-ovi tai oven kautta poistuvien henkilöiden määrä on yli 60.

In English (our rendering): Doors must open in the direction of escape if more than 60 people leave through the door.

### FI-1009-2017-9: outdoor air per person

- Document: Indoor climate and ventilation of new buildings, decree 1009/2017
- Clause: 9 § Ulkoilmavirrat
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: classroom, office, hall, any occupied room
- Requirement: 6 dm³/s per person
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/1009 (copy: `sources/fi_1009_2017.xml`)

> Oleskelutilojen ulkoilmavirraksi on mitoitettava vähintään 6 dm³/s henkilöä kohti suunniteltuna käyttöaikana, jos tilan käyttötarkoituksesta ei aiheudu lisäilmavirran tarvetta.

In English (our rendering): Occupied spaces must be designed for at least 6 dm³/s of outdoor air per person during the designed period of use.

### FI-241-2017-4: door clear width

- Document: Accessibility of buildings, government decree 241/2017
- Clause: 4 § Ovet
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: door to any room, corridor
- Requirement: 850 mm on an internal route; 800 mm into rooms
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/241 (copy: `sources/fi_241_2017.xml`)

> Rakennuksen sisäisellä kulkuväylällä olevan oven ja aukon [...] vapaan leveyden on oltava vähintään 850 millimetriä.

> Muun rakennuksen kuin asuinrakennuksen huonetiloihin [...] johtavan oven ja kulkuaukon vapaan leveyden on oltava vähintään 800 millimetriä.

In English (our rendering): A door or opening on an internal route must have a clear width of at least 850 mm. A door into a room of a non-residential building must have at least 800 mm.

### FI-241-2017-5: turning space on a narrow route

- Document: Accessibility of buildings, government decree 241/2017
- Clause: 5 § Rakennuksen sisäinen kulkuväylä
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: corridor
- Requirement: route under 1,500 mm wide needs a 1,500 mm turning space every 15 m
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/241 (copy: `sources/fi_241_2017.xml`)

> Jos 1 momentissa tarkoitettu kulkuväylä on leveydeltään alle 1 500 millimetriä, kulkuväylällä on oltava vähintään 15 metrin välein kääntymistila, jonka halkaisija on vähintään 1 500 millimetriä.

In English (our rendering): If the route is narrower than 1,500 mm, it must have a turning space of at least 1,500 mm diameter at least every 15 metres.

### FI-241-2017-10: accessible toilet

- Document: Accessibility of buildings, government decree 241/2017
- Clause: 10 § Wc-tila muussa kuin asuinrakennuksessa
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: toilet, accessible toilet
- Requirement: 1,500 mm free circle; 800 mm free on each side of the seat
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/241 (copy: `sources/fi_241_2017.xml`)

> Edellä 1 momentissa tarkoitetussa wc-tilassa on oltava halkaisijaltaan vähintään 1 500 millimetriä vapaa tila. [...] Lisäksi wc-istuimen kummallakin puolella on oltava vapaata tilaa vähintään 800 millimetriä.

In English (our rendering): The accessible toilet must have a free space of at least 1,500 mm diameter, and at least 800 mm free on each side of the seat (or two toilets side by side, one free on the left and one on the right).

### FI-241-2017-3: landing in front of the entrance door

- Document: Accessibility of buildings, government decree 241/2017
- Clause: 3 § Rakennuksen sisäänkäynti
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: entrance, lobby
- Requirement: 1,500 mm by 1,500 mm
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/241 (copy: `sources/fi_241_2017.xml`)

> Ulko-oven edessä olevan tasanteen on oltava vähintään 1 500 millimetriä leveä ja vähintään 1500 millimetriä pitkä.

In English (our rendering): The landing in front of an exterior door must be at least 1,500 mm wide and 1,500 mm long.

### FI-1008-2017-4: minimum room area and height

- Document: Dwelling, accommodation and work spaces, decree 1008/2017
- Clause: 4 § Asuin-, majoitus- ja työtila
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: office, staff room, any work space
- Requirement: 7 m² and 2.5 m
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/1008 (copy: `sources/fi_1008_2017.xml`)

> Asuin-, majoitus- ja työtilan huonealan on kuitenkin aina oltava vähintään 7 neliömetriä. Asuin-, majoitus- ja työtilan huonekorkeuden on oltava vähintään 2,5 metriä.

In English (our rendering): A dwelling, accommodation or work space must always have a room area of at least 7 m² and a room height of at least 2.5 m.

Limit: Applies to dwelling, accommodation and work spaces. Whether a classroom counts as a work space under this decree was not checked.

### FI-1008-2017-5: window size

- Document: Dwelling, accommodation and work spaces, decree 1008/2017
- Clause: 5 § Asuin-, majoitus- ja työtilan ikkuna
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: office, any work space
- Requirement: light opening at least 1/10 of the room area
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/1008 (copy: `sources/fi_1008_2017.xml`)

> Jos työtilassa on oltava ikkuna, valoaukon on oltava vähintään 1/10 työtilan huonealasta.

In English (our rendering): If a work space must have a window, its light opening must be at least one tenth of the room area.

### FI-1007-2017-4: stair riser and going in an exit

- Document: Safety in use of buildings, decree 1007/2017
- Clause: 4 § Sisäportaiden mitoitus
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: stair
- Requirement: riser at most 180 mm, going at least 270 mm
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/1007 (copy: `sources/fi_1007_2017.xml`)

> Uloskäytävässä portaan askelman nousu voi olla enintään 180 millimetriä. Etenemän on oltava vähintään 270 millimetriä.

In English (our rendering): In an exit, the riser of a stair may be at most 180 mm and the going must be at least 270 mm.

### FI-1007-2017-7: guard rail

- Document: Safety in use of buildings, decree 1007/2017
- Clause: 7 § Kaide
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: stair, balcony, any level change
- Requirement: required above a 0.5 m drop; 1.0 m high (1.2 m above a 6 m drop)
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/1007 (copy: `sources/fi_1007_2017.xml`)

> Rakennuksessa tai sen lähiympäristössä on oltava kaide, kun putoamiskorkeus ylittää puoli metriä ja putoamisen tai harhaan astumisen vaara on olemassa, eikä toiminnan luonne edellytä kaiteettomuutta.

In English (our rendering): A guard rail is required where the fall height exceeds half a metre and there is a risk of falling.

### FI-796-2017-6: speech intelligibility

- Document: Acoustic environment of buildings, decree 796/2017
- Clause: 6 § Ääniolosuhteet
- Status: law
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: classroom, hall, office, dining
- Link: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2017/796 (copy: `sources/fi_796_2017.xml`)

> Rakennuksen, jossa on potilashuoneita, opetus-, kokous-, ruokailu-, hoito-, harrastus-, liikunta- tai toimistotiloja, ääniolosuhteet on suunniteltava ja toteutettava siten, että tilassa saavutetaan sen käyttötarkoitus huomioon ottaen riittävä puheenerotettavuus.

In English (our rendering): In buildings with teaching, meeting, dining, care, hobby, sports or office spaces, acoustic conditions must give sufficient speech intelligibility for the use of the space.

Limit: Sets a goal, not a number. It cannot be checked from a floor plan.


## USA: the clauses

### US-ADA-404.2.3: door clear width

- Document: 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design
- Clause: 404.2.3 Clear Width
- Status: federal law (enforceable standard)
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: door to any room
- Requirement: 32 inches (815 mm)
- Link: https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/2010-stds/ (copy: `sources/us_ada_2010_standards.html`)

> Door openings shall provide a clear width of 32 inches (815 mm) minimum.

### US-ADA-403.5.1: accessible route width

- Document: 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design
- Clause: 403.5.1 Clear Width
- Status: federal law (enforceable standard)
- Checked against: official text

- Requirement: 36 inches (915 mm)

> Except as provided in 403.5.2 and 403.5.3, the clear width of walking surfaces shall be 36 inches (915 mm) minimum.

### US-ADA-304.3.1: turning space in a toilet room

- Document: 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design

- Status: federal law (enforceable standard)
- Checked against: official text
- Applies to: toilet, accessible toilet
- Requirement: 60 inches (1525 mm) diameter
- Link: https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/2010-stds/ (copy: `sources/us_ada_2010_standards.html`)

### US-ADA-604.3.1: clearance around the water closet

- Status: federal law (enforceable standard)

### US-IFC-907.2.3-WA: fire alarm in schools

- Document: International Fire Code as adopted by Washington State (WAC 51-54A)
- Clause: 907.2.3 Group E
- Status: state law (Washington); model code elsewhere
- Checked against: state's published text
- Applies to: any room in a school, classroom, kitchen, corridor
- Requirement: manual fire alarm system; not required at an occupant load of 50 or less
- Link: https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=51-54A (copy: `sources/us_wa_wac_51-54A-0907.htm`)

> Group E occupancies shall be provided with a manual fire alarm system that initiates the occupant notification signal [...]

> A manual fire alarm system is not required in Group E occupancies with an occupant load of 50 or less.

Limit: This is Washington State's amended wording. Other states adopt the model code with their own changes; the model code publisher's site refused automated access, so the unamended text was not checked.

### US-IFC-1004.5-WA: occupant load: floor area per person

- Document: International Fire Code as adopted by Washington State (WAC 51-54A)
- Clause: Table 1004.5 Maximum floor area allowances per occupant
- Status: state law (Washington); model code elsewhere
- Checked against: state's published text
- Applies to: classroom, kitchen, workshop
- Requirement: classroom 20 net sq ft (1.86 m²); shops and vocational rooms 50 net; commercial kitchens 200 gross
- Link: https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=51-54A (copy: `sources/us_wa_wac_51-54A-1004.htm`)

> Kitchens, commercial 200 gross
